# Sources and provenance

Abyssal Siren Jinx 3.0.1 is a derivative League skin mod assembled from locally installed Riot assets and original authored additions. No generated images are used.

## Shipped asset inputs

- Ocean Song Jinx, skin 65: destination BIN/resource topology, native 106-joint skeleton, animation compatibility, and the 319 known-good particle-card textures.
- Jinx skin 51: anatomy, face, and hair foundation for the champion model.
- Jinx skin 62: Pow-Pow and Fishbones weapon cores and compatible sea-creature component material.
- Authored for this project: coral crown and armor, shell/relic weapon additions, Zapper treatment, Chompers and ultimate-missile geometry, recall geometry, and opaque body, armor, weapon, recall, mine, and missile atlases.
- Dark-witch SFX donors: Coven Evelynn, Coven Morgana, and Sunken Shadows Nami. Their media is rerouted across all 60 Ocean Song skin-65 SFX events and 63 referenced media files. Voice-over is not included.

The installed `Jinx.wad.client` is the authoritative build input. Source skin assets are retargeted onto the unchanged skin-65 skeleton and resource paths; they are not installed as additional champion skins.
